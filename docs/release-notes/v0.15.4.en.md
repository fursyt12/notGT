### What's new in 0.15.4

- **No frame goes missing at the start of a title.** When the NDI sender's timer ran a
  few milliseconds late — likelier on Windows — it could drop a frame of an animation
  that had just started, most often its second. A frame whose turn has come is now
  always sent.
- **The sender says where its frames go.** With **Detailed NDI and Spout sender log**
  switched on in the launcher, each out now also says, every two seconds, how many frames
  Chromium painted, how many were replaced while the converter was busy, sent new or
  again, dropped by the frame clock, or skipped by Spout while a receiver held the
  picture; and the log names the GPU and its driver. `NOTGT_NDI_TRACE=1` logs every frame
  as it goes — the log to send when frames go missing.
- **A test of title starts.** In the package's `ndi` folder,
  `..\node\node.exe scripts\bench.mjs --render gpu --page entry --fps 60 --shows 40`
  checks that every show reaches an NDI receiver from its first frame, every frame of
  it. With `--real http://127.0.0.1:9090 --token <apiToken>` it shows a test title on
  your own notGT over the API, as an operator would, and checks the same. Close the
  launcher's sender first, and have the NDI runtime installed.
