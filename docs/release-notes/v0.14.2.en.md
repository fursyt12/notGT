### What's new in 0.14.2 (beta)

- **The WebGL2 converter works again.** Since 0.14.0 it stopped on the first frame —
  “totals is not defined”, then “Illegal invocation” — and the NDI sender fell back to
  the CPU. Both were slips in 0.14.0's new frame clock; WebGL2 now converts at a steady
  60 fps again, with the same steady latency as the native converter (26 ms at 1080p60
  on a Radeon 780M), and tests run the clock the way a page does.
- **A converter that has to give up is never left half-done.** If even the next one
  cannot start, the out says so in the dashboard and tries again, instead of staying on
  the converter that stopped. When Chromium hands over pictures with nothing in them,
  the sender says so rather than sending nothing in silence.
- **`--render gpu` draws on the GPU even where it is emulated,** as its help says (the
  default, `auto`, still chooses the CPU there).
