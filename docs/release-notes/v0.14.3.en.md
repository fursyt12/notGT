### What's new in 0.14.3 (beta)

- **Every NDI out in a process of its own.** All outs used to share one Chromium GPU
  process, which drew every page on one thread: four outs of heavy code titles at
  1080p60 got only 13–20 new frames a second each, with most of the computer idle. The
  NDI sender now runs each out in an Electron of its own, with its own GPU process and
  cores: the same four outs got 48–50 new frames a second each on a Radeon 780M (an
  integrated GPU, by then 85% busy) and latency fell from 160–240 ms to 81–103 ms. An
  out that crashes is started again by itself, with the reason under the out in the
  dashboard; the others carry on. Each out costs about 0.7–1.2 GB of memory;
  `--one-process` (or `NOTGT_NDI_ONE_PROCESS=1`) keeps them in one, for many light outs
  on a small machine.
- **The GPU converters never freeze.** Under heavy load the WebGL2 converter could wait
  for the GPU for ever and repeat one frame on every out. A frame now has two to three
  seconds; past that the converter is given up, with its reason, and the next takes over.
