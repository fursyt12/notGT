### What's new in 0.15.2

- **Glows and particles look right over Spout.** The Spout sender used to convert the
  out's picture to straight alpha, dividing each colour by its transparency. Resolume and
  TouchDesigner take a Spout picture as premultiplied, so every faint pixel — the soft
  edge of a glow, a fading particle — showed at full colour: glows came out as solid
  bright blobs, where an OBS browser source showed them soft. The picture now goes out
  exactly as Chromium draws it. Outs with transparency switched off are unchanged.
- **0.15.1** made Spout a library of its own (`spout/`), put out-page timers back on the
  server's clock, and gave each out's process its own Chromium profile — see the
  [0.15.1 notes](https://github.com/fursyt12/notGT/releases/tag/v0.15.1).
