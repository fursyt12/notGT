### What's new in 0.15.0

- **Spout on Windows.** Every out now has a **Spout** switch under the NDI one in
  **Titles & Outs**. Switched on, the out becomes a Spout sender on the Windows computer
  that runs notGT — `notGT <out>` unless you name it — and Resolume, TouchDesigner, OBS
  with the Spout2 plugin and other Spout programs on that computer list it as a source.
  The picture never leaves the GPU: Chromium's frame is drawn straight into the texture
  Spout receivers open, at the out's size, with no compression and nothing on the
  network. The settings give the name, the frame rate (60 fps unless set) and whether the
  transparency goes with it. Spout needs no NDI runtime; the launcher starts everything,
  so with the Windows package there is nothing to install. An out can go out over NDI and
  Spout at once.
- **Every NDI out in a process of its own.** All outs used to share one Chromium GPU
  process, which drew every page on one thread: four outs of heavy code titles at
  1080p60 got only 13–20 new frames a second each, with most of the computer idle. Each
  out now runs in an Electron of its own, with its own GPU process and cores: the same
  four outs got 48–50 new frames a second each on a Radeon 780M, and latency fell from
  160–240 ms to 81–103 ms. An out that crashes is started again by itself, with the
  reason under the out in the dashboard; the others carry on. Each out costs about
  0.7–1.2 GB of memory; `--one-process` (or `NOTGT_NDI_ONE_PROCESS=1`) keeps them in one.
- **The GPU converters never freeze.** Under heavy load the WebGL2 converter could wait
  for the GPU for ever and repeat one frame on every out. A frame now has two to three
  seconds; past that the converter is given up, with its reason, and the next takes over.
- **Since 0.12.7** (the betas 0.14.0–0.14.2): steady NDI latency, right colours at every
  NDI size, drawing on the GPU where there is a real one, faster conversion on the CPU,
  and a native NDI converter to try (`NOTGT_NDI_CONVERTER=native`). See the
  [0.14.0](https://github.com/fursyt12/notGT/releases/tag/v0.14.0),
  [0.14.1](https://github.com/fursyt12/notGT/releases/tag/v0.14.1) and
  [0.14.2](https://github.com/fursyt12/notGT/releases/tag/v0.14.2) notes.
