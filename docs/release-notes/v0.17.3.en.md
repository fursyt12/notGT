### What's new in 0.17.3

- **No more lyrics of another song.** When no source had the track by its artist, notGT
  Lyrics Bridge took the song of the same title and about the same length by anyone — meant
  for a name written in the other alphabet, it put KayBlack's "Bonjour" on
  "whyvsevolod — Bonjour". A song by another artist is now taken only when the two names
  may be one in Cyrillic and Latin letters ("Фараон" and "Pharaoh"); Musixmatch's matches
  are judged the same way. A track whose lyrics no source has stays without them.
- **Artists from LRCLIB by majority.** LRCLIB's credits are typed in by whoever uploaded a
  copy, writers included; the credit most copies agree on counts now, so "Кино" stays
  "Кино" and does not become "Кино, В. Цой".
  Download the new `notgt-lyrics-bridge.exe` from this page.
