### What's new in 0.17.4

- **A new track's lyrics come at once.** From some networks (Russian providers, since 2025)
  a connection to Cloudflare, where LRCLIB lives, freezes after its first ~16 KB: the answer
  begins and never ends. notGT Lyrics Bridge waited for every such request until its
  timeout and then threw the half answer away, so the lyrics of a new track took 3–10 s to
  show, and sometimes never did. Now each LRCLIB request has a connection of its own, an
  answer that stops is taken as far as it came, and the title gets what LRCLIB finds answer
  by answer: the first line is there in about 0.3 s.
- **A new track is noticed sooner:** Spotify is asked five times a second instead of twice.
  Download the new `notgt-lyrics-bridge.exe` from this page.
