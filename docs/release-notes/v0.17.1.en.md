### What's new in 0.17.1

- **Corner Pin.** A new tool in the Editor's toolbar, next to **`Crop`** and
  **`Scale Bounds`**: four round handles on the corners of the selected animation, each
  moved on its own, and the animation is warped after them in perspective — a title on a
  screen or a wall seen at an angle. The canvas shows the warp exactly as it goes on air,
  for animations built from layers and for code animations alike; only the new shape is
  drawn, not the rectangle it came from. A corner sticks to the out's lines and to other
  animations and stops where the shape would fold over itself; moving and scaling the
  placement carry the warp along, and **`Reset`** takes it off. Through the API it is the
  placement's `corners` (`{tl, tr, br, bl}` in template pixels; `null` removes it).
- **notGT Lyrics Bridge is a download of its own.** The program that sends the lyrics of
  what Spotify plays to the **Karaoke** title — `notGT Karaoke.exe` in 0.17.0 — is now
  `notgt-lyrics-bridge.exe`, one file on the release page next to the zips, no longer
  inside the Windows package: download it onto whichever computer plays the music and
  connect it to your notGT. Its settings moved with the name, so type the address and the
  WebSocket key once more.
