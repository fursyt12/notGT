### What's new in 0.12.6

- **A video on its own gets a box of its own size.** A new animation made
  “From layers” starts as a copy of the lower third (1040×184). With its
  layers deleted and a video layer added, the clip used to be squeezed into
  that strip. Now, when the uploaded or picked clip is the animation's only
  layer, the animation's box becomes the clip's own size — scaled down to
  the out when the clip is larger (a 4K clip on a 1080p out gives
  1920×1080), never up — and the clip fills it. With other layers present
  the animation keeps its box and the video layer takes the clip's
  proportions inside it, as before.
